Ten Pillars of Enterprise AI Modernization

Comprehensive systems engineering for commercial client acquisition, autonomous revenue operations, conversational voice triage, and bespoke enterprise LLM deployments.

01
Client Acquisition Engine

Pay-Per-Appointment Commercial Acquisition

Vetted decision-maker consultations injected directly into your reps' calendar schedules. Pricing applies strictly to confirmed, qualified appointments satisfying written qualification criteria.

Key Problems Solved

  • Skyrocketing lead acquisition costs with weak downstream qualification.
  • Senior account executives wasting time on unvetted calls.
  • Commercial pipeline leaking due to sluggish manual lead follow-up.

Architectural Workflow

  1. Multi-Channel Ingestion: Capture high-intent paid & inbound buyers.
  2. Multi-Layer AI Screening: Verify budget, title authority, and timeframe.
  3. Direct Calendar Injection: Real-time two-way calendar sync with briefing dossiers.
  4. Show-Up Protocols: Omnichannel SMS, WhatsApp, and email attendance sequences.
02
Cognitive Enterprise Software

Custom Enterprise AI Systems

Bespoke intelligence engines fitted directly onto your proprietary databases, ERP workflows, and commercial proposal pipelines.

Key Problems Solved

  • Off-the-shelf SaaS tools failing to address proprietary workflow bottlenecks.
  • High-friction document processing slowing down RFP and proposal delivery.
  • Sensitive IP leaking via untrusted external commercial AI tools.

Architectural Workflow

  1. Ingestion Gateway: Secure processing of PDFs, contracts, and financial records.
  2. Structured Extraction: LLM parsing of line items, terms, and margin thresholds.
  3. Validation Engine: Automatic cross-referencing against historical pricing tables.
  4. Automated Proposal Output: Generates client-ready deliverables and updates ERP.
03
Omnichannel AI

Conversational AI Agents & Inbound Setters

Autonomous multi-turn qualification agents operating 24/7 across web chat, WhatsApp Business API, and SMS with sub-second response times.

Key Problems Solved

  • Inbound web traffic abandoning due to delayed rep response times.
  • Expensive human live-chat teams unable to cover overnight and global timezones.
  • Inconsistent qualification answers from fragmented junior sales reps.

Architectural Workflow

  1. Sub-Second Activation: Welcomes prospect instantly upon form or chat trigger.
  2. Dynamic Discovery Dialogue: Unpacks requirements, timeline, and budget.
  3. Interactive Booking: Offers real-time rep calendar slots directly in the chat.
  4. HubSpot / CRM Logging: Complete chat transcript mapped to deal stages.
04
Speech Intelligence

Enterprise AI Voice Agents

Sub-450ms conversational telephony agents handling high-volume outbound re-activation calls, inbound triage, and schedule confirmation calls with natural speech cadence.

Key Problems Solved

  • Dormant lead databases remaining untouched due to outbound rep labor costs.
  • Inbound switchboards overwhelmed during peak promotion campaigns.
  • High appointment no-show rates from lack of pre-meeting voice confirmations.

Architectural Workflow

  1. Telephony Dialing Engine: High-throughput SIP trunking with strict STIR/SHAKEN trust.
  2. Streaming Speech-to-Text: Real-time neural voice synthesis with adaptive turn-taking.
  3. Objection Resolution: Guided branch logic resolving timing and pricing queries.
  4. Call Recording & Sentiment Analysis: Automatic briefing notes delivered to sales rep.
